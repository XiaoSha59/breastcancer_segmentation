Suggested manuscript wording:
  LCC-collapse and opposite-image-half/spatially displaced component selection were
  strongly associated, but not identical. Opposite-half displacement accounted for
  part of LCC-collapse cases, while remaining collapse cases reflected additional
  component-selection or fragmentation failure modes. These results support
  reporting LCC as a sensitivity analysis rather than a universally safe clinical
  postprocessing rule.

Important caution:
  The opposite-half flag is a heuristic based on image-half centroids, not a confirmed
  anatomical laterality label. Use wording such as 'opposite image half' or 'large
  spatial displacement', not 'wrong breast', unless manually confirmed.
